What My Notebook Knows That I Don’t

A meditation on ink, avoidance, and the eleven drafts of a single paragraph.

There is a particular kind of silence that only happens at a desk at five in the morning, when the house is asleep and the only sound is a nib dragging across paper. I used to think that silence was empty. It turns out it was full of sentences waiting for me to stop talking.

This is the essay where I try to explain why I keep a notebook I never reread, why the good pen matters more than it should, and what a quiet person is actually doing when he says he is just thinking.
